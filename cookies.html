Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We use cookies on the Studio Style Pro website to optimize performance, manage sessions, and collect anonymous traffic statistics.

1. Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the website to run and do not require user consent:

2. Performance & Analytics Cookies

We use these cookies only if you click "Accept All" on our cookie banner:
