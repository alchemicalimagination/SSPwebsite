Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Studio Style Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of salon owners ("Users") and their clients. This Privacy Policy details how we process and safeguard information collected through our website and B2B salon management platform.

1. Data Controller & Registration

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018:

  • Data Controller: Margherita Iaconeta (Sole Trader)
  • Business Address: 80A Ruskin Ave, Welling, London DA16 3QQ, United Kingdom
  • Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) Registration: ZC187008
  • Support Email: support@studiostylepro.com

2. Data We Process

As a B2B platform, we act as a Data Processor for the salon client data you input, and a Data Controller for your registration data:

  • Account Information: Business name, owner name, email address, password, phone number, and Stripe billing records.
  • Operational Data: Appointments, service records, employee rosters, and inventory logs.
  • Sensitive Data Note: Allergy notes or technical treatment details entered by salon employees are held securely. You must ensure you have appropriate client consent to store these records.

3. How We Share & Store Data

We store data securely in servers located in the UK/EEA. We only utilize sub-processors who adhere to strict security agreements:

  • Supabase Inc.: Core database hosting (encrypted in transit and at rest).
  • Stripe Inc.: Card processing and subscription billing. We do not store full credit card details.
  • Sentry: Real-time error detection and app crash monitoring.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your account details, as well as request a copy of your operational data. If you wish to delete your account or exercise your data rights, email us at support@studiostylepro.com.
